This calculator gives an estimate for planning purposes only, based on Sections 236C, 236K, and 37 (Capital Gains Tax) of the Income Tax Ordinance as amended by the Finance Act 2025, plus commonly reported provincial stamp duty and registration fee rates as of mid-2026. Stamp duty, registration fee, mutation/town tax, and DC valuation rates change by province, property type, and specific notification — confirm current rates with your local sub-registrar, Excise & Taxation Department, or the CDA before a transaction. Non-filer Capital Gains Tax is taxed at your normal income slab rate (minimum 15%), which this tool approximates rather than computes exactly. Nothing here is tax or legal advice; for an actual transaction, consult a tax practitioner or property lawyer.
