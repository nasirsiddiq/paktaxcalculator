Apna Ghar Program Calculator

Estimate your monthly rental (installment) under the Government of Pakistan's Wazir-e-Azam Apna Ghar Program — Ghar Ho Tu Apna, a subsidized Islamic housing finance scheme offered by Meezan Bank and other participating Islamic banks under the Diminishing Musharakah mode of financing. This calculation follows Islamic banking (Shariah-compliant) principles — it is based on rental and profit-sharing under a joint-ownership partnership, not interest (riba), which this scheme does not involve or promote.

1. Property & Your Contribution

Minimum customer contribution is 10% of the property value. Maximum bank financing is PKR 10 million or 90% of property value, whichever is lower.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Apna Ghar Program?

The Wazir-e-Azam Apna Ghar Program — Ghar Ho Tu Apna is a Government of Pakistan profit-subsidy and risk-sharing housing finance scheme. Participating Islamic banks offer financing under it through the Diminishing Musharakah mode, with the government subsidizing part of the rental rate to keep it at 5% for the first 10 years.

What is Diminishing Musharakah?

It's a Shariah-compliant partnership: the bank and customer jointly buy the property. The customer pays periodic rent on the bank's share (which is progressively bought back), so the bank's ownership share — and the rent charged on it — shrinks every period until the customer owns the property outright.

Why does my installment change after 10 years?

The 5% rental rate is a fixed, subsidized rate that applies only for the first 10 years of the financing. For any remaining tenure beyond that, the rate switches to a variable rate of KIBOR + 3%, which moves with the State Bank of Pakistan's benchmark rate. This calculator lets you plug in an assumed future KIBOR to estimate that later installment.

How is the maximum financing amount determined?

The bank will finance whichever is lower: 90% of the property's value, or PKR 10 million. If 90% of your property's price is higher than Rs. 10 million, you'll need to contribute more than the 10% minimum out of pocket to make up the difference.

Who is eligible?

Applicants (and co-applicants, if income is being clubbed) generally need a minimum gross monthly income of PKR 40,000, must be between 18 and 65–70 years old at financing maturity depending on the clubbing arrangement, and must meet minimum employment/business tenure requirements. Full eligibility, required documents, and city restrictions should be confirmed directly with your bank.

Is this calculator affiliated with any bank or the Government of Pakistan?

No. This is an independent estimation tool built from publicly available program terms. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any participating bank, the State Bank of Pakistan, or the Government of Pakistan. Always confirm your exact payment plan and eligibility directly with a participating bank.
