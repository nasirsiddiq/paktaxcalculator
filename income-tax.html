Based on Section 82 of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001: (a) present 183+ days in the tax year; (b) present 120+ days in the tax year and 365+ days cumulatively in the preceding 4 tax years; (c) a Federal/Provincial Government employee posted abroad; or (d) a Pakistani citizen not resident/183+ days present in any other country in the tax year (added by the Finance Act 2022, aimed at Pakistanis not tax-resident anywhere). Meeting any one test is sufficient for residency. This is a simplified guide — edge cases (part-year moves, tax treaties, tie-breaker rules) should be confirmed with a tax advisor or FBR.
