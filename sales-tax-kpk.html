Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Sales Tax on Services

Provincial sales tax on services in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, administered by the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Revenue Authority (KPRA) under the KP Finance Act (Sales Tax on Services). Select a service category and enter the value excluding tax.

The default/standard rate for services in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is 15% under the KP Finance Act (Sales Tax on Services). This calculator additionally reflects the specific rate changes notified via amendments to the KP Sales Tax on Services Act 2022's First & Second Schedules (notification dated May 2026) for a limited set of categories — rental/leasing, real estate, foreign exchange dealing, motor vehicle insurance, IP licensing, publishing, health-related services, digital-payment restaurant/hotel bills, and annual developmental projects. We were not provided the full, consolidated First & Second Schedule for KP, so categories not explicitly listed above should be assumed to fall under the 15% standard rate, but please confirm your exact tariff heading and current rate with the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Revenue Authority (KPRA) or a tax advisor, as KPRA revises schedules periodically. For withholding on the GST portion of an invoice, see Sales Tax Withholding.
