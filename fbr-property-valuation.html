For cities marked Verified, the locality-level rates in Step 2 are transcribed directly from that city's official FBR notification (S.R.O.) — the source and notification date are shown with each result so you can cross-check. For all other cities, FBR's tables are large, change periodically, and are only published as PDFs, so the Step 3 calculator only does the arithmetic (rate × size, with unit conversion) on whatever number you copy from the official PDF yourself. Either way, verify you've matched the correct locality, road/block, and property category before relying on the result. Nothing here is tax or legal advice.
