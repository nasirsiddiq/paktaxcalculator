This uses the silver standard for Nisab (the more precautionary threshold most scholars use), except when gold is your only zakatable asset — then Nisab is assessed by gold's own weight (7.5 tola / 87.48g), not its market value, per the Hanafi position. As soon as any other zakatable wealth is added, everything is combined and measured against the silver-value standard again. Zakat is calculated on the current value of your wealth at the time you calculate/pay it — not its value on the day you first reached Nisab.

Live and historical exchange, gold and silver rates are fetched from a third-party public API (frankfurter.dev) at the moment you click a "Fetch Rates" button. They are provided for convenience and may occasionally be unavailable or slightly delayed — always spot-check against another source before paying a large amount of Zakat. This tool gives a general estimate for guidance only and is not a religious ruling (fatwa). Rules on jewelry, business valuation, and specific asset classes vary between schools of thought — please consult a knowledgeable scholar or your local imam for your specific situation.
