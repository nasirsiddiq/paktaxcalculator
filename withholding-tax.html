WHT under the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 has many category-specific rates and exemptions beyond what is listed here. Most rates shown are minimum/adjustable tax (creditable against final liability); dividend and profit-on-debt rates are generally final tax. TY2025-26 rates are sourced from FBR's official Withholding Tax Rate Card updated to 30 June 2025 (Finance Act 2025); TY2026-27 rates reflect Finance Act 2026 changes. Both tax years show the same 14 categories, but the line items inside a category can differ, because some charges did not exist in both years — social-media/digital platform revenue (s.154B) and life-insurance payout gains (s.151B) were introduced by the Finance Act 2026 and therefore have no TY2025-26 equivalent, while the graduated property-transfer slabs under s.236C and s.236K were replaced by flat rates from TY2026-27. Note also that FBR had not published an official TY2026-27 rate card at the time of writing, so TY2026-27 figures are drawn from professional-firm commentary on the Finance Act; the non-filer rates for s.236C and s.236K in particular are not officially confirmed and are shown on the assumption that the previous non-filer rates carry over. Verify the exact clause applicable to your transaction with FBR's official rate card or a tax advisor.
