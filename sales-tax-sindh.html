Sindh Sales Tax on Services

Provincial sales tax on services in Sindh, administered by the Sindh Revenue Board (SRB) under the Sindh Sales Tax on Services Act 2011. Select a service category and enter the value excluding tax.

Rates are sourced from the SRB "Working Tariff" (amended up to 10 July 2024), which lists both the statutory rate under the Second Schedule to the Sindh Sales Tax on Services Act, 2011 and the effective/reduced rate currently in force via SRB notifications. Where a category shows both a "standard" and a "reduced/election" rate, the reduced rate typically requires the registered person to file a specific election form and/or use SRB-linked POS/e-invoicing — if you haven't made that election, the standard rate applies. Many categories also carry additional conditions (turnover thresholds, input tax credit restrictions, franchise/branch exclusions) not captured in this simplified calculator. Confirm your exact tariff heading and eligibility with the Sindh Revenue Board (SRB) or a tax advisor. For withholding on the GST portion of an invoice, see Sales Tax Withholding.
